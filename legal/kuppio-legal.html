Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Kuppio ("Kuppio," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Kuppio mobile app — a local discovery app for cute coffee shops, home-based food sellers, and hidden food spots. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Kuppio, you agree to the practices described here.

I.

Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and password (stored securely and never in plain text) to create and secure your account.

Location. With your permission, we collect your device's GPS location to show you nearby spots, calculate distances, and center the map near you. You can deny or revoke location access at any time in your device settings; the app will still work, using an approximate default location instead.

Photos and media. If you choose to add a photo or video — to a review, a post, or a business listing — we collect and store that file. If you submit a business for verification, we also collect a photo of a government-issued ID and a photo of the business/storefront; these two verification photos are stored privately and are only visible to you and to Kuppio's review team, never to other users.

Content you create. Reviews, posts, comments, saved spots, collections, and business listings you create are stored so they can be shown to you and (where public-facing, like reviews and posts) to other users.

Camera and microphone. If you record a video post, we access your camera and microphone only for that recording, with your permission.

Usage and device information. We may collect basic technical information (such as device type and app version) to diagnose problems and keep the app working correctly.

II.

How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Operate core features: showing nearby spots, displaying and ranking reviews and posts, letting you save spots and place preorders
  • Verify business ownership claims before granting control of a business listing
  • Review reports of content or users that violate our guidelines
  • Maintain and improve the app, and diagnose technical issues

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

III.

How we store and share information

Your data is stored using Supabase, our backend infrastructure provider, which hosts our database and file storage. Supabase acts as a data processor on our behalf and does not use your data for its own purposes.

Reviews, posts, comments, and public business listings are visible to other users of the app, as is inherent to how those features work. Your government-ID and business-verification photos are never shown publicly — only to Kuppio's review team, for the sole purpose of confirming a business claim.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Kuppio, our users, or the public.

IV.

Your choices

  • Delete your account. You can permanently delete your account and associated personal data at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app. Businesses you manage are unclaimed (not deleted) so the listing can continue to exist for other users; your personal reviews, posts, comments, and saved items are removed.
  • Block and report. You can block another user to stop seeing their content, and report content or users that violate our guidelines.
  • Location permission. You can deny or revoke location access at any time in your device's Settings app.
V.

Children's privacy

Kuppio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the information below and we will delete it.

VI.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
